## Synthesis

- Support/resistance evidence indicates structural levels can exhibit temporary memory and decay, which motivates preserving root identity across time rather than recreating roots every session.
- Dynamic support/resistance methods explicitly adapt levels as market state changes; adaptation itself does not prove a new independent information root.
- Time-varying moving-average and regime research demonstrates that rolling/adaptive transforms move mechanically with changing windows and volatility.
- Financial structural-break research shows abrupt changes can materially alter time-series behavior, but ex-post break estimation is not equivalent to a live observable regime change.
- Change-point and regime models therefore require predictor-time firstObservableAt semantics before D01 may use them to alter structural-root treatment.
