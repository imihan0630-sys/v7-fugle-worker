Updated: 2026-10-07 Asia/Taipei

## 1. Purpose

DL-063 separated structural response from overnight information, previous-close anchoring and pre-open price discovery.

DL-064 freezes the next Taiwan-specific attribution firewall:

> A response near an old support/resistance zone after a limit-up/limit-down session may reflect the daily price-limit mechanism, magnet behavior, delayed price discovery, unmet same-day order imbalance, or next-session resubmission rather than incremental structural memory.

A price-limit boundary is an exchange constraint, not a structural support/resistance level.

No return outcome is opened in this tranche.

## 2. Official Taiwan market rules

TWSE ordinary stocks are generally constrained to 10 percent above/below the current-session opening-auction reference price, subject to explicit exceptions such as the first five trading days of some newly listed common stocks.

Order priority follows price priority and time priority.
At the same price:
- pre-opening order priority is randomly arranged by computer;
- after opening, time priority follows order entry sequence.

Trading orders are valid only for the session in which they are placed.
Therefore a physical unexecuted order from day t does not automatically persist as the same order into day t+1.

This distinction is mandatory.

## 3. Evidence context

Taiwan high-frequency evidence finds a statistically/economically significant tendency for prices to accelerate toward the upper daily limit, with weaker evidence near the lower bound.

Taiwan price-limit studies also document:
- delayed price discovery;
- overnight continuation following limit moves;
- subsequent intraday correction/reversal;
- trading-volume/imbalance displacement into later periods.

Therefore proximity to, contact with, or next-day movement after a price limit can be generated by the limit mechanism itself.

This is an alternative explanation, not Pattern alpha evidence.

## 5. Three different carryover concepts

### C0 — PHYSICAL_ORDER_QUEUE_SAME_SESSION

Actual unexecuted orders visible/verified in day t order-book data.

This queue exists only within that session.

### C1 — LATENT_UNMET_DEMAND_OR_SUPPLY_CARRYOVER

Economic buying/selling pressure may persist after close.

It is not the same physical order queue.

It must be inferred/tested prospectively from next-session behavior and owner-certified flow/imbalance receipts.

### C2 — NEXT_SESSION_RESUBMITTED_ORDERS

New orders entered for day t+1.

These are new exchange orders with new priority.

Pre-opening same-price priority is randomly assigned by the matching system.

Hard rule:

PHYSICAL_ORDER_QUEUE_OVERNIGHT_PERSISTENCE =
FALSE.

These states are descriptive context, not strength scores.

## 8. "Locked limit" requires evidence

Do not infer "locked limit" from close price alone.

A stock may close at the upper/lower limit while:
- trading volume occurred at the bound;
- orders were intermittently executable;
- it unlocked/relocked;
- the final visible queue differed materially from intraday state.

Use only owner-certified order-book / execution-path evidence.

Without it:
CLOSE_AT_LIMIT_QUEUE_UNKNOWN.

## 9. Queue size is not structural strength

Verified queue/imbalance may indicate:
- temporary order imbalance;
- news-driven demand;
- trend-following;
- strategic order placement/cancellation;
- liquidity shortage;
- price-limit magnet effect.

It is not automatically:
- support strength;
- resistance strength;
- independent confirmation.

No LIMIT_QUEUE_STRENGTH_SCORE is defined.

## 10. Limit magnet vs structural attraction

Near a daily upper/lower bound, price can accelerate because traders anticipate the constraint.

Therefore a structural zone close to the daily limit can be confounded by the exchange boundary.

Store:

- currentPriceToLimitDistanceTicks;
- structureLimitOverlap;
- limitDirection;
- firstLimitHitAt;

No arbitrary "near limit" threshold is frozen by D01.

## 14. Order validity / queue expiration firewall

Official order validity is session-limited.

Therefore:
- day-t queue receipt expires with the session;
- day-t+1 order-book state is a new receipt;
- matching priority resets under next-session rules.

Permitted statement:
"unmet demand may persist economically."

Prohibited statement:
"the same queue carried overnight."

## 18. Limit move and delayed price discovery

Future D16 analysis must distinguish:

A. MAGNET_OR_TRADING_INTERFERENCE
- price accelerates toward the bound / trading constraint affects path.

B. DELAYED_PRICE_DISCOVERY
- next-session continuation reflects incomplete day-t adjustment.

C. OVERREACTION_CORRECTION
- later intraday reversal reflects prior constrained/overreacted move.

D. STRUCTURAL_RESPONSE_RESIDUAL
- zone effect remains after limit-mechanism controls.

No one path is assumed ex ante.

## 19. News/event confounding

A limit hit can be driven by:
- earnings/news;
- corporate action;
- policy;
- sector/global shock;
- rumor/information arrival.

D08 owner receipt remains mandatory where available.

The limit mechanism is a market constraint, not the underlying information cause.

## 20. Queue cancellation / spoofing caution

Displayed queue is not immutable demand.

Orders can be canceled/reduced/repriced according to exchange rules.

D01 therefore stores timestamped queue snapshots.

Do not use end-of-day queue as if it described the entire limit-hit interval.

Do not infer trader intent from queue alone.
