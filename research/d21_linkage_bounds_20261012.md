# D21 linkage-selection bounds (synthetic only)

For N known filing events, K verifiably linked outcomes and E linked events with transfers, the full-filing-population binary transfer incidence lies between E/N and (E+N-K)/N. This does not identify exchange sales or insider intent.

Example: N=100, K=60, E=45 gives 45% to 85%, despite a 75% rate among linked cases. The 40 unlinked events are UNKNOWN, not 0. This bound is not applicable to the whole market if the initial filing population N is incomplete.

Hypothesis: linked-only performance generalizes to all filings. Counterexample: missing links may depend on delisting, resignation, transfer method, revisions and coverage. Alternative: coverage rather than behavioral signal. Failure condition: no stable OOS incremental effect after conservative bounds, year/industry/size/controller/market-state stratification, date-cluster and costs.
