# D01 DL-062 — Structural Response vs Opening/Closing Auction and End-of-Session Liquidity V0.1

Updated: 2026-10-07 Asia/Taipei
Status: CLASS_A_RESEARCH_ONLY / OUTCOME_BLIND / AUCTION_MICROSTRUCTURE_FIREWALL / FORMAL_CORE_LOCKED

## 1. Purpose

DL-061 separated structural response from index-weight, passive-flow, ETF and constituent-arbitrage mechanics.

DL-062 freezes another Taiwan-specific microstructure attribution firewall:

> A price response near a support/resistance zone at the open or close may reflect call-auction clearing, benchmark/index replication, month-end/rebalance demand, indicative-auction revisions or end-of-session liquidity concentration rather than an incremental structural-memory effect.

Opening and closing call auctions are not equivalent to continuous intraday trading.

No future return outcome is opened in this tranche.

## 2. Taiwan market-structure evidence

TWSE official rules state:
- pre-open orders accumulate before the opening call auction;
- regular trading is continuous during the day;
- orders accumulate for a call auction during the last five minutes before the close;
- closing prices are widely used for portfolio valuation and index calculation;
- simulated transaction price/volume and bid/ask information are disseminated during pre-open and pre-close periods;
- closing matching may be delayed under extreme indicative-price changes.

Taiwan empirical research finds:
- the five-minute closing call auction changed closing volatility, efficiency and liquidity;
- transparency changes in the closing call altered order aggressiveness and closing-price behavior;
- month-end closing behavior changed after the transparency reform.

International evidence also shows closing-auction volume is strongly linked to indexing, ETF ownership and rebalance events.

These facts motivate an attribution firewall, not a Pattern alpha claim.

## 4. Session-phase states

Freeze the following states:

S0 CONTINUOUS_TRADING_UNCONSTRAINED

S1 PREOPEN_INDICATIVE_ONLY
- simulated/indicative state before the first opening print;
- not a completed trade.

S2 OPENING_CALL_FINAL_PRINT
- first matched trade generated by opening call auction.

S3 CLOSING_CALL_INDICATIVE_ONLY
- simulated/indicative state during the last five-minute call;
- not the final closing trade.

S4 CLOSING_CALL_FINAL_PRINT
- final closing-auction print.

S5 CLOSING_CALL_DELAYED
- closing matching postponed under the exchange rule.

S6 POST_CLOSE_FIXED_PRICE
- after-hours fixed-price trading at the same-day closing price;
- not a new continuous price-discovery event.

S7 SESSION_PHASE_UNKNOWN
- data or clock cannot resolve phase.

## 5. Indicative vs executed price

Hard rule:

INDICATIVE_PRICE != EXECUTED_PRICE.

Pre-open or pre-close simulated prices/volumes may change before the final auction.

They may be used only as auction-state context when:
- receipt timestamp is known;
- predictorFreezeAt is later than the receipt;
- no later indicative update is silently substituted.

The final opening/closing print may not be backfilled into an earlier predictor snapshot.

## 6. Closing-window mixed-bar firewall

A bar spanning both:
- continuous trading before 13:25;
- closing call auction ending at 13:30;

is:
AUCTION_MIXED_BAR.

It may not be treated as a pure continuous-trading bar for:
- touch identity;
- breakout confirmation;
- retest confirmation;
- volume confirmation;
- wick/body interpretation

unless the underlying trade/session data allow phase separation.

Aggregated OHLC alone cannot infer which portion of the bar came from auction mechanics.

## 7. Opening-gap firewall

The opening print aggregates overnight/pre-open orders.

A zone crossed by:
- prior close below;
- opening call print above;

is not equivalent to a continuous intraday crossing through the zone.

Freeze:
OPENING_GAP_CROSSING.

D01-05 breakout lifecycle must preserve this distinction.

No intraday path through the zone may be invented.

## 8. Closing-auction touch/crossing

If the only zone touch/crossing occurs in the closing call final print:
CLOSING_AUCTION_ONLY_INTERACTION.

This is a valid observed price interaction, but it is not equivalent to a continuous-auction-free interaction.

Future inference must compare:
- structural opportunities away from auction;
- otherwise comparable opportunities at the close.

## 9. Closing benchmark / index replication

Closing price is economically important because it can determine:
- portfolio valuation;
- index levels;
- benchmark tracking;
- ETF NAV-related marks;
- derivatives/settlement contexts where applicable.

Therefore end-of-day order concentration may be benchmark-driven.

Preserve:
- index rebalance effective day;
- constituent add/delete/weight change;
- month-end/quarter-end;
- derivative expiry/settlement if owner-certified;
- passive-flow receipt;
- ETF primary-market receipt;
- benchmark-close targeting receipt.

Do not infer passive execution from high closing volume alone.

## 10. Closing liquidity concentration

Store owner-certified:
- closeAuctionVolume;
- closeAuctionVolumeShare;
- preCloseSpread/depth;
- auction imbalance/indicative imbalance if available;
- last-five-minute liquidity state;
- continuous-session volume baseline;
- same-symbol historical auction-volume baseline;
- market-wide auction concentration.

D01 defines no arbitrary "high closing volume" threshold.

## 11. Auction price-pressure vs structural response

Primary comparator:

G0 AUCTION_OR_CLOSE_MECHANICAL_EVENT_AWAY_FROM_ZONE
- same symbol/session-type or common-support context;
- auction/passive/rebalance/liquidity concentration present;
- no structural opportunity at the target zone.

G1 AUCTION_OR_CLOSE_MECHANICAL_EVENT_AT_ZONE
- same mechanical context;
- valid structural opportunity at the zone.

If G1 adds no residual representation over G0, auction/close mechanics are sufficient.

## 12. Opening comparator

Separate comparator family:

O0 OPENING_CALL_EVENT_AWAY_FROM_ZONE

O1 OPENING_CALL_EVENT_AT_ZONE

Do not pool opening and closing calls automatically:
- information set differs;
- overnight gap risk differs;
- benchmark demand differs;
- liquidity composition differs.

## 13. Time-order firewall

Required invariant:

mechanicalReceiptKnownAt <= predictorFreezeAt < endpointWindowStart.

For indicative auction state:
indicativeKnownAt <= predictorFreezeAt.

For final print:
finalPrintKnownAt <= predictorFreezeAt only if the predictor is formed after execution.

If finalPrintKnownAt > predictorFreezeAt:
POST_FREEZE_AUCTION_PRINT_NOT_ELIGIBLE.

## 14. Auction delay / volatility interruption

If closing matching is delayed:
- scheduled close timestamp is not the execution timestamp;
- later execution must carry its actual knownAt;
- any predictor frozen before the delayed print cannot use the delayed final price.

State:
CLOSING_AUCTION_DELAYED.

Do not backdate the print to 13:30.

## 15. Month-end / rebalance state

Month-end and rebalance days may have systematically different closing demand.

Preserve:
- calendar month-end;
- quarter-end;
- index rebalance announcement/effective states;
- benchmark weight changes;
- passive-flow model state;
- actual execution if verified.

No event label automatically means mechanical causality.
It is context requiring D16 comparison.
