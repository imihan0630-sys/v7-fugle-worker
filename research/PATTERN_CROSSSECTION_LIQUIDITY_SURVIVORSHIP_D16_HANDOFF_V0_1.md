# D01 DL-039 — D16 Cross-Section Liquidity / Size / Survivorship Handoff V0.1

Updated: 2026-10-05 Asia/Taipei
Status: RESEARCH_ONLY / OUTCOME_JOIN_CLOSED

## 1. Purpose

D01 freezes the cross-sectional population and attrition semantics.

D16 owns future economic inference.

The central question is:

> Does Pattern generalize beyond the large, liquid, long-listed, data-rich subset that is easiest to observe and trade?

## 2. Two permissible target populations

POINT_IN_TIME_MARKET_UNIVERSE:
- market-wide historical claim only if survivorship-safe point-in-time membership is verified.

FORMAL_ELIGIBLE_UNIVERSE:
- legitimate system-relevance study;
- claim scope limited to existing Formal eligibility.

Never silently move from the second to a market-wide claim.

## 3. Required attrition ledger

For every target-universe symbol/date preserve:
- history ready;
- history too short by design;
- history/data blocked;
- detector no structure;
- structure emitted;
- no valid opportunity;
- opportunity blocked;
- economic evaluable;
- tradability evaluable.

UNKNOWN is not failure and is not dropped.

## 4. Listing age

Use listing-age-aware expected history.

A newly listed symbol with complete available history but insufficient detector lookback is:
HISTORY_TOO_SHORT_BY_DESIGN.

It is not:
- missing data;
- no pattern;
- negative pattern.

## 5. Survivorship

Historical universe must include later-delisted members when they were point-in-time eligible.

Future delisting information may not enter the predictor.

D16 later freezes terminal-return/censoring policy.
D01 does not assign a delisting return.

## 6. Size/liquidity adjustment

Future inference reports point-in-time:
- size;
- value traded / volume / turnover;
- spread/depth where available;
- relative tick;
- listing age;
- market;
- price-limit/constraint state.

Use canonical owner receipts.

No current-value historical backfill.

## 7. Detection vs tradability

Estimate and report separately:
- probability of history/data readiness;
- detector evaluability;
- structure emission;
- valid opportunity;
- economic evaluability;
- tradability/cost evaluability.

A strong economic result among tradable cases cannot erase earlier selection stages.

## 8. Common support

Pattern/control or structural groups require overlap in:
- size;
- liquidity;
- listing age;
- price/tick tier;
- market;
- regime.

No outcome-driven trimming.

## 9. Concentration

Report:
- unique symbols;
- roots;
- opportunities;
- top contributor shares;
- preregistered concentration statistic.

Many opportunities from one symbol are not broad cross-sectional replication.

## 10. External evidence interpretation

Taiwan evidence that technical-trading performance varies with size/volume means these variables are genuine cross-sectional context.

Taiwan evidence linking size behavior with illiquidity / price-limit constraints further strengthens the need for explicit support.

Survivorship literature requires restored historical membership rather than current-survivor reconstruction.

These are controls, not proof of Pattern alpha.
