## Purpose

Pre-register a Taiwan-specific control for the possibility that a visually important resistance/support level overlaps a psychologically salient or mechanically coarse round price.

This control exists to prevent the Pattern lane from falsely attributing all historical-level behavior to chart memory when part of the mechanism may be limit-order clustering around round prices.

It is a CONTROL / CONFOUND descriptor only.

It is NOT:
- a score;
- a veto;
- a bullish/bearish signal;
- a new R09 factor;
- a reason to alter Formal A/B ranking;
- evidence that every round price is a real barrier.

## External mechanism evidence

### Chiao & Wang, Financial Review, 2009
"Price Clustering: Evidence Using Comprehensive Limit-Order Data"
DOI: 10.1111/j.1540-6288.2008.00208.x

Using comprehensive TWSE limit-order data, the study reports:
- clustering at integer/even prices;
- stronger clustering in nonmarketable orders;
- strategic front-running around clustered queues;
- evidence that clustering can create price barriers.

Important portability limit:
- sample is 2005-2006 under the old 7% daily-limit / frequent-call-auction regime.

Therefore this is a mechanism witness, not a current-2026 effect-size estimate.

### Lien, Hung & Hung, Journal of Empirical Finance, 2019
"Order price clustering, size clustering, and stock price movements: Evidence from the Taiwan Stock Exchange"
DOI: 10.1016/j.jempfin.2019.03.005

The study reports:
- price/size clustering across TWSE investor types;
- price tails concentrated at zero/five;
- clustering varies with tick size and transitory volatility;
- strategic one-tick-ahead behavior under order competition.

Important portability limit:
- evidence predates the 2020-03-23 continuous-trading reform.

### Current structural regime
TWSE currently uses continuous intraday trading between the opening and closing call auctions.
The daily price-limit and tick-grid rules are also material to what constitutes a legal "round" quote.

Therefore old clustering evidence must not be transported as a fixed 2026 penalty.

## Core identification problem

Observed rejection around a historical major zone can be generated by a mixture of:

1. historical-price memory / anchoring;
2. live order-depth clustering near salient round prices;
3. stop / breakout orders around that price;
4. trend / momentum / attention;
5. tick-size mechanics;
6. daily price-limit censoring;
7. ordinary noise.

Pattern research must not call mechanism (1) proven when (2)-(6) remain uncontrolled.

## Why retain a small anchor hierarchy instead of one chosen grid

The Taiwan literature documents multiple forms of clustering:
- integer;
- even;
- zero/five tails.

Choosing one single anchor family after looking at Pattern outcomes would be data snooping.

Therefore v0.1 stores the small literature-grounded hierarchy without weights or a selected winner.

If later outcome analysis compares these four proximity families, they count as separate registered variants for multiple-testing accounting.

No additional 20/50/100-NTD grids may be added merely because they look good on examples.
A new grid requires a separately documented hypothesis before outcomes.

## Tick-normalized interpretation

Absolute NT$ distance is not comparable across price levels.

Primary descriptive comparison:
distance in legal Taiwan stock ticks.

Secondary:
percentage distance.

At high price levels, the current legal tick itself may be several NTD.
If a candidate round anchor collapses to an almost automatic property of the tick grid, report:
MECHANICALLY_COARSE_GRID = true

Do not interpret such proximity as behavioral clustering.
