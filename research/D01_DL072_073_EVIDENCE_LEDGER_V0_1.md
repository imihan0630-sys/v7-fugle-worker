## Research evidence

- Support/resistance literature documents measurable memory/reversal behavior at previously identified levels, supporting the hypothesis that trader coordination can matter.
- Limit-order-book resiliency research shows depth/spread can recover after aggressive market orders and that replenishment intensity is state dependent.
- Other microstructure research shows adverse selection and liquidity imbalance can cause passive liquidity withdrawal or different order placement.
- Liquidity-crisis evidence shows book depletion can amplify price moves nonlinearly.
- These findings imply repeated touches can either reinforce apparent structure or consume/withdraw liquidity depending on state; touch count alone cannot identify direction.
- Call-auction and sparse-liquidity evidence from prior D01 tranches remains applicable to touch deduplication and zone uncertainty.
