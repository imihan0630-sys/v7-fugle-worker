# D01 DL-078 — Evidence Ledger V0.1

Updated: 2026-10-07 Asia/Taipei
Status: CLASS_A_RESEARCH_ONLY / SOURCE_LINEAGE_FROZEN

Research synthesis:
- Support/resistance research supports the existence of temporary structural memory, but does not imply that several nearby chart-derived objects constitute several independent causal signals.
- Technical-analysis research routinely derives moving averages, support/resistance, momentum and other indicators from the same price history; this creates a clear redundancy risk.
- Research on large technical-rule families and cherry-picked indicators reinforces the need to control multiplicity and overlapping signal information.
- Therefore geometric clustering is treated as topology first and evidence count second.
