# D07-33 frozen checks

Input: pre-R&D result 150, research 60, development 40, no tax, no other capex. Capitalized development is not yet available for use in year 1.

Case A: EBIT 90, CFO 90, investing development cash -40, total cash 50.
Case B: EBIT 50, CFO 50, investing development cash 0, total cash 50.
Checks: EBIT gap 40; CFO gap 40; total-cash gap 0. A CFO-only free-cash proxy overstates comparability by 40 if development investment is omitted.

Year 2-6: A amortizes 40/5=8 annually. Holding other inputs fixed, annual EBIT gap is -8; 40-5*8=0 cumulative accounting gap. Different recognition eligibility does not imply equal future economic value.
