## Why no generic sample-size shortcut

The D16 promotion gate rejects a universal fixed-N rule for regime/policy studies because:
- dates can belong to one persistent episode;
- rows on one date are dependent;
- state occupancy can be sparse;
- effect size and cost uncertainty differ by module.

Therefore the Wave-1 program reports:
- parent/event rows;
- distinct clean dates;
- episode counts;
- support retention;
- UNKNOWN/exclusion share;
