Updated: 2026-10-07 Asia/Taipei

Research synthesis:
- Support/resistance research documents path-dependent temporary price barriers and measurable price memory, so episode-based state representation is reasonable.
- Optimal-stopping work on support/resistance treats entry/exit as linked path-dependent decisions, reinforcing the need to separate structural information from timing decisions.
- Technical-rule research shows large rule families are vulnerable to data snooping and costs, supporting deduplication and predeclared policy comparison rather than raw signal counting.
- Recent research on cherry-picked trend signals argues that multiple complex indicators can represent overlapping information and increase selection risk.
- Therefore breakout, retest, reclaim, continuation and trend confirmation must not be granted independent votes merely because their labels differ.
