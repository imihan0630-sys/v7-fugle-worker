# D01 DL-074 — Evidence Ledger V0.1

Updated: 2026-10-07 Asia/Taipei
Status: CLASS_A_RESEARCH_ONLY / SOURCE_LINEAGE_FROZEN

Relevant research synthesis:
- Technical trading profitability can disappear after transaction costs in multiple empirical studies, so geometric RR cannot substitute for executable RR.
- Optimal-stopping work with transaction costs and stop-loss constraints demonstrates that stop level and take-profit level are jointly dependent decision variables rather than independent decorations.
- Support/resistance optimal-stopping research supports formalizing entry/exit boundaries, but does not validate one universal stop/target rule for Taiwan equities.
- Therefore D01-11 should preserve gross geometric RR separately from execution-adjusted RR and must prevent the same structural uncertainty from being applied repeatedly.
