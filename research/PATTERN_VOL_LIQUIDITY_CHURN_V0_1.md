## 1. Purpose

DL-049 separated zone occupancy from directional churn/path disorder.
DL-050 separated ordinary continuous transitions from auction, VI, price-limit, event and bid-ask-bounce mechanics.

DL-051 addresses a remaining confound:

> High zone churn may reflect volatility clustering, widening spreads, thinning depth or time-of-day market-quality effects rather than structural-zone information.

The same transition count can have different meaning under different volatility and liquidity states.

No directional alpha sign is assumed.
No future-return outcome is opened in this tranche.

## 2. External evidence

Taiwan market-quality evidence shows that volatility and liquidity dimensions move together and materially affect order behavior.

- Lien, Hung, Zhu and Chen (2019) report that after Taiwan price-limit changes, time-weighted spreads and intraday volatility increased while market depth decreased.
- Lien, Hung and Lo (2022) show transitory volatility, bid-ask spread, market depth and trading time significantly affect order choices in TWSE data.
- Taiwan tick-size / intraday market-quality evidence documents U-shaped spread/volatility patterns and inverted-U depth patterns across the trading day.

These findings imply that observed local churn can be partly generated by market-quality state rather than structural geometry.

They do not prove that volatility/liquidity explains D01 alpha.

## 6. Timing split

Microstructure context must be separated by causal timing.

### PRE_EPISODE_CONTEXT

Available before the zone-interaction/churn episode begins:
- trailing volatility;
- spread;
- depth;
- session position;
- liquidity regime.

These may be baseline context variables.

### WITHIN_EPISODE_CONTEXT

Observed during the churn episode:
- realized volatility generated during the episode;
- spread widening during the episode;
- depth depletion during the episode;
- quote-update bursts.

These can be contemporaneous mechanisms or mediators.

They are not automatically baseline confounders.

## 8. Volatility clustering caution

A volatility burst can mechanically increase:
- state transitions;
- zone crossings;
- cumulative travel;
- bar-span frequency.

Therefore raw churn count is not scale-invariant.

Future D16 work must compare:
- raw churn;
- volatility-normalized churn/path descriptors;
- common-support subsets in volatility state.

D01 does not prescribe the normalization estimator beyond using owner-provided PIT volatility scales.

## 9. Spread widening caution

A wider bid-ask spread can:
- increase observed transaction-price alternation;
- enlarge one-tick / one-quote price moves;
- make trade-price churn exceed midpoint churn.

Therefore where quote data exist, retain separately:
- trade-price zone state;
- midpoint / quote-reference zone state;
- quoted spread;
- effective spread where canonical.

Do not infer midpoint path from OHLC bars.

## 10. Depth deterioration caution

Thin market depth can make small order flow move price through a structural zone more easily.

Store owner-provided depth metrics / regimes.

Do not interpret high transition count under depleted depth as stronger structural rejection/acceptance without control.

Depth is a liquidity context, not an independent D01 vote.

## 11. Quote staleness

Spread/depth data require quote freshness.

If quote age exceeds the canonical owner's validity contract:
QUOTE_CONTEXT_STALE.

Stale quote context is not zero spread / zero depth.
It makes microstructure adjustment NOT_EVALUABLE.
