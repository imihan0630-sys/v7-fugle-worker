## Research question

Should Market Regime（市場環境） change:
1. strategy activation;
2. strategy-local ranking;
3. global admission priority;
4. only monitoring intensity;
5. or none of the above?

Do not assume one universal answer.

## Why no ranking bonus is implemented now

Current System 2 regime source is incomplete:
- TAIEX current context exists;
- TPEx / small-cap regime is not yet frozen to an equivalent PIT-safe contract;
- breadth and sector rotation are prospective/partial;
- large-vs-small leadership is incomplete;
- global/macro remains UNKNOWN without canonical durable receipts.

A ranking rule such as "Risk-on +10" or "Risk-off -10" would therefore be both premature and semantically coarse.

## Strategy-specific hypotheses

### SHORT_MOMENTUM（短線動能）
Plausible hypothesis:
trend/breadth/sector participation may improve continuation quality.

Counter-hypothesis:
strong idiosyncratic/event/sector momentum can work during weak broad markets, so a broad Risk-off veto may destroy useful opportunities.

### SWING_GROWTH（波段成長）
Plausible hypothesis:
supportive liquidity/regime may accelerate repricing.

Counter-hypothesis:
fundamental repricing may begin before broad regime improves, so regime may affect timing/exposure rather than thesis rank.

## Rejection rule

Reject/downgrade regime priority if:
- it adds no incremental value beyond stock-level factors;
- it merely restates sector/price-volume evidence;
- it works only in a few crisis dates;
- it increases missed-opportunity cost in early recovery;
- source coverage selects only easy dates/segments.
