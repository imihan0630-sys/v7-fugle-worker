# System 2 RANK-06 Multi-Strategy Overlap Experiment V0.1

Updated: 2026-09-27 Asia/Taipei
Status: PREREGISTERED RESEARCH QUESTION / NO OVERLAP BONUS / DESCRIPTIVE REDUNDANCY RECEIPT FIRST

## Research question

When one symbol independently qualifies under multiple strategies, does that provide incremental evidence that deserves higher global priority?

Do not assume yes.

## Core risk

A naive rule such as:
"2 strategies = twice as strong"
is prohibited.

Two strategy labels can still be driven by the same underlying evidence.

Example:
SWING_GROWTH（波段成長） + INDUSTRY_TREND（產業趨勢）
may both depend heavily on INDUSTRY_THESIS（產業投資邏輯）.

Likewise:
SHORT_MOMENTUM（短線動能） can be a later-stage price state of an earlier growth/industry thesis rather than a fully independent mechanism.

## V0.1 overlap receipt

Before any ranking bonus, freeze descriptive overlap between strategy contracts.

For each strategy pair record:
- PRIMARY / REQUIRED evidence-family sets;
- shared core families;
- distinct core families for each strategy;
- all-family overlap;
- setup/interaction references where available;
- source-readiness constraints;
- whether both strategy memberships were independently valid at the same decision timestamp.

The overlap receipt is descriptive.
It does not output "stronger stock".

## Why no hard overlap threshold

V0.1 does not define:
- "Jaccard > X means redundant";
- "one distinct family means independent";
- "+N points per extra strategy".

Those thresholds would be arbitrary before outcome evidence.

Numeric overlap ratios may be stored as diagnostics, not as ranking scores.

## Research arms once prospective data exists

A. No overlap bonus.
B. Naive strategy-count ordering.
C. Distinct-family overlap context.
D. Distinct-family overlap after interaction/redundancy controls.
E. Strategy-pair-specific interaction.

Mandatory comparison:
B must not be promoted merely because it is simpler.
C/D must show incremental value beyond the strongest individual strategy membership.

## Required outcomes

For symbols with one versus multiple valid memberships:
- trigger conversion;
- profitable-trigger conversion;
- MFE / MAE;
- D1/D3/D5/D10 and strategy-appropriate longer horizons;
- time-to-trigger;
- invalidation rate;
- capacity occupancy;
- industry/regime/date concentration.

Pair analyses must remain strategy-pair specific.

## Falsification

Reject a multi-strategy priority effect when:
- the benefit disappears after controlling for shared evidence families;
- the strongest single strategy explains the entire effect;
- the effect exists only for one pair or one market regime;
- strategy-count bonus increases concentration/crowding without better outcomes;
- source completeness is systematically higher for multi-strategy names.

## Current decision

Multi-strategy membership remains explainability/context only.
No global admission bonus, no active-monitor bonus and no exposure bonus are authorized.

First build overlap/redundancy receipts and collect prospective common-support evidence.
