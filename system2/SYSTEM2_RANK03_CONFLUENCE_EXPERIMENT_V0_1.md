## Research question

Does an explicit CONFLUENCE（共振） interaction add incremental ordering value beyond its component evidence families?

Do not assume yes.

## Candidate interactions

SHORT_MOMENTUM（短線動能）:
`BREAKOUT_ACCEPTANCE_CONFLUENCE（突破接受共振）`

SWING_GROWTH（波段成長）:
`GROWTH_REPRICING_CONFLUENCE（成長重新評價共振）`

These names already exist in the owner-approved Confluence Engine and strategy contracts, but the interaction itself is not yet proven to add incremental information.

## Why RANK-03 is not activated yet

A naive "confluence present = rank higher" rule would violate the research discipline because:
- the interaction may simply restate its component families;
- technical structure and price-volume can be correlated;
- growth/catalyst/industry evidence may partially describe the same earnings-revision mechanism;
- current interaction observations lack a dedicated PIT/redundancy research receipt in the runtime.

Therefore RANK-03 ranking promotion is blocked until interaction observations can freeze:
- decision timestamp;
- component references;
- PIT eligibility;
- interaction state;
- redundancy-control state;
- falsification tag;
- version.

## Required interaction states

Observation state:
- KNOWN
- UNKNOWN
- STALE
- INVALID
- NOT_APPLICABLE

Confluence state:
- SUPPORTIVE
- NEUTRAL
- ADVERSE
- INDETERMINATE

Redundancy state:
- NOT_TESTED
- CONTROLLED_FOR_RESEARCH
- REDUNDANCY_WARNING
- REJECTED_REDUNDANT

An interaction with UNKNOWN/STALE/INVALID inputs may not be promoted as supportive.

## Coverage rule

Interaction evidence must report coverage before performance.

A tiny subset with complete event/industry/technical data cannot be compared against a broad baseline without common-support restriction.
